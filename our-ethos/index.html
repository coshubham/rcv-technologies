Ethos: explore our event journey

Team building is an important part of any successful business, and corporate events play an incredible role in it. Our Ethos section showcases the vibrant culture that defines us: the activities, events and unforgettable parties.

21stories from our culture

At RCV Technologies we believe in nurturing both the personal and professional growth of our team. That is why our event calendar is a dynamic mix of cultural celebrations and technical gatherings: Diwali, Lohri, Christmas and New Year parties, foundation days in Alwar and Jim Corbett, a fashion week, a filmy Friday, fitness months, NGO visits and blood donation drives, alongside hackathons, workshops and tech talks.

Cultural events bring the team together in a tapestry of traditions, fostering understanding and companionship across backgrounds. Technical events keep us at the forefront of industry trends and encourage continuous learning. Here we share stories, photos and insights from each of them, reflecting our commitment to a balanced, engaging workplace. Explore the latest posts and glimpse the heart of RCV Technologies.
