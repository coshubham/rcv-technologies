Project

Fits a paid media audit before a media plan is locked, a Q4 campaign launch that has to be live by a set date, or a GA4 migration ahead of a reporting deadline. Written scope, one point of contact, delivery on the committed date. Media budget, if any, is separately quoted and buyer-owned.

Retainer

Fits growth teams that need always-on campaign management, weekly creative refresh, and continuous optimization rather than one-off pushes. The best digital marketing services in India run on this shape because performance channels reward consistency, not sprints. You would get: monthly deliverable set, media pacing report, monthly review call, quarterly business review.

Dedicated resource

The role is scoped to what the buyer is short on: a paid media buyer, a growth analyst, a content strategist, or a GA4 and attribution lead. Reports into the buyer's marketing lead, uses the buyer's tools, sits inside the buyer's Slack. From the best digital marketing company in India, without the recruiting cycle or the fully loaded cost.

Fits agencies that want to offer paid, SEO, content, and analytics as one bundled service without hiring four specialists in-house. One contract, one delivery lead on the RCV Technologies side, four channel outputs branded as the partner's white-label digital marketing agency.

Transparency

Dashboards pull directly from Google Ads, Meta, GA4, and CRM sources, not from a middleman spreadsheet. No hidden markups on media. No blended fee that obscures where the money went. How we deliver the best digital marketing services in India

The model exists so nothing depends on a single person’s memory or a shared inbox. Governance
and reporting are foregrounded, not added at the end.

Scope and discovery

The engagement opens with a 60-minute scoping call to align on the buyer's target. A delivery lead is assigned before the call ends. The scoped document lands within three business days, covering channel priorities, monthly deliverables, media budget assumptions, success metrics per channel, and the escalation path. Nothing starts, and no media is bought, without a signed contract.

What you receive: Written scope document, named delivery lead, kick-off call scheduled, first-week creative and channel brief.

Audit and baseline

Every digital marketing engagement begins with a baseline audit so improvement is measured against a real starting point, not a claim. The audit stack includes an account-structure and creative audit across every active paid channel, and a tracking and attribution audit. Whatever the buyer has been paying for, we can price against what it produced.

What you receive: Written audit, baseline metrics per channel, prioritized action list scored by impact and effort.

Execution

Certified specialists on the RCV Technologies bench, part of one of the top digital marketing agencies in India by delivery discipline. Paid media buyers hold a maximum client count, growth analysts a maximum reporting load, so nobody is spread across ten campaigns at once. Weekly campaign standups keep pacing and creative on track.

What you receive: monthly deliverables per the scope, weekly campaign updates, creative refreshes on cycle, from named specialists.

Reporting and review

Every month closes with a written report against the metrics. Every quarter closes with a quarterly business review that looks past the monthly ups and downs at trend, cost per outcome by channel, and where the budget should shift next quarter.

What you receive: monthly written report with channel-level cuts, monthly review call, quarterly business review with channel-mix recommendation.

Iteration

The scope is not fixed for the life of the engagement, and the channel mix is not either. At every quarterly review, budget can be reallocated between paid and organic, between paid social and paid search, or between acquisition and retention, based on what the numbers are showing.

What you receive: Scope and budget-mix adjustment options at every quarter, no lock-in beyond the contracted term.
