Off-the-shelf software forces your team to work the way the software works. A custom web application does the opposite. RCV Technologies builds portals, dashboards, CRMs, booking engines and SaaS products that mirror your processes exactly, remove manual steps and give management the numbers in real time.

We start every build with discovery workshops and clickable prototypes, so you see the application before a line of production code is written. Engineering follows with a clean architecture, Laravel, Symfony or Django on the server, React or Vue on the front end, automated tests and documented APIs, which is what keeps the product easy to extend two years from now.

Security, role-based access, audit logs and backups are standard, not add-ons. After launch our team stays on for monitoring, enhancements and scaling, so the application grows with the business it was built for.

Our custom web application development process

01

Discovery & planning

We begin by sharing their needs and thereafter provide a detailed insight of their options. This allows us to determine what should be included in the project and what technologies would guarantee timely delivery of the project.

02

UI/UX design

Our design team employs simplicity, order, and pleasant visual layouts to make it simple for the user to operate an application. Our strategy tends to involve the accessibility and the usability of our site, specifically, the engagement factor.

03

Development

To develop the application and make it real, we use the concepts of application development life cycle and employing our personnel, who is our developer, we adopt the agile development method. Our full-stack developers guarantee the application is designed efficiently with quality source code compiled for enhanced update and constant maintenance.

04

Quality assurance & testing

Prior to going live, practice and check marks have been made, including functional testing, performance, and security testing as performed by our QA team.

05

Deployment & maintenance

When done, we then move your application to a production server. After the application has been delivered to the client, we offer ongoing support, improvements and enhancement to ensure that your application is up to date with the latest innovation for optimal functionality.
