When you need to be on both app stores and the budget or deadline will not stretch to two native teams, cross-platform development is the answer. RCV Technologies builds Flutter and React Native apps that share up to ninety percent of their code while delivering native look, feel and speed on each platform.

Cross-platform app development

Cross-platform development is ideal for businesses seeking to maximize their mobile presence across diverse device ecosystems without the cost and time commitment of separate native apps. with a single codebase, cross-platform apps allow for consistent user experiences across iOS and Android, fast deployment, and simplified maintenance—all while providing a native-like experience for end users.

Our Cross-Platform development approach

Our cross-platform app development approach emphasizes high performance, seamless design, and robust functionality. Leveraging the latest tools and technologies, we create applications that integrate smoothly across devices, enhancing user engagement and expanding your brand’s reach.

Efficient development with React native and Flutter

Our developers are experts in React Native and Flutter, two leading frameworks for cross-platform development. With React Native’s JavaScript-based architecture and Flutter’s Dart programming language, we ensure your app delivers optimal responsiveness, performance, and a native-like look and feel across platforms.

Single codebase for multiple platforms

We build a single codebase that functions effectively on both iOS and Android, simplifying development, reducing time-to-market, and minimizing maintenance efforts. This approach enables rapid updates and ensures that features and functionality remain consistent across devices.

Native-Like performance and ui/ux

Through careful optimization, we achieve near-native performance with fluid UI/UX. Using platform-specific design components and libraries, our team delivers a smooth, engaging user experience that closely resembles native app interactions.

Integrated APIs and Device-Specific functionality

Our cross-platform apps support advanced integrations such as camera, GPS, and accelerometer services. We leverage plugins and APIs that adapt seamlessly across operating systems, ensuring your app’s features perform consistently and reliably on any device.

Key Cross-Platform development features

Frameworks: React Native, Flutter

By using frameworks that enable efficient cross-platform development, we deliver applications that combine the power of native features with the flexibility of a single codebase.

Unified User Experience Across Platforms

We adhere to each platform’s design guidelines—Material Design for Android and Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines for iOS—to create cohesive user experiences across devices while maintaining a consistent brand identity.

Reduced Development Time and Costs

With a unified development approach, we accelerate the deployment process and reduce expenses. This method also streamlines maintenance, as updates and improvements can be applied across platforms simultaneously.

Advanced Functionalities with Adaptable Plugins

Our cross-platform solutions incorporate sophisticated functionalities, such as push notifications, in-app purchases, and device hardware access, ensuring that the app is equipped with essential and engaging features regardless of the platform.

Our Cross-Platform app development process

01

Consultation & strategy

We begin by analyzing your project requirements, audience demographics, and desired functionalities. Our team guides you in choosing the optimal development approach to achieve both platform compatibility and budget goals.

02

UI/UX design

Our design team creates intuitive, visually appealing interfaces that align with both Android and iOS design standards. By leveraging platform-specific design principles, we ensure a user-friendly experience that feels natural on any device.

03

Cross-Platform development

Our engineers use React Native or Flutter to develop your application from a single codebase, integrating essential features, APIs, and security protocols. This unified approach streamlines development while preserving functionality and responsiveness.

04

Comprehensive testing & optimization

To ensure consistent performance across devices, we conduct extensive testing on both iOS and Android. Our QA team performs usability, functionality, and performance testing to confirm the app runs smoothly across platforms.

05

Deployment & maintenance

Once the app is fully tested, we guide you through deployment on both the App Store and Google Play Store, ensuring all guidelines are met. Post-launch, we offer ongoing support and updates to keep your app performing at its best.
