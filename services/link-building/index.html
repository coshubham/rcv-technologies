Five pillars of link building delivery

Project

Fixed-scope, fixed-price link building services work tied to a dated milestone. Fits a targeted campaign around a new page launch, a competitor gap close, or a Google penalty recovery. Written scope covers tactic mix, publisher DR range, placement count, anchor text plan, and go-live window. Any publisher-side placement fees are quoted separately, disclosed to the buyer, and never marked up.

Retainer

Monthly link building services output at capped scope, ongoing for the contract term. The best link building service in India runs on retainers because profile shape compounds only with rhythm: fifteen well-placed links a month against a designed profile outperforms eighty scattered links crammed into a quarter. Buyer gets: monthly placement set, live-URL log, DR and referring-domain progression report, quarterly profile-shape review.

Dedicated resource

A named specialist embedded into the buyer's SEO team with monthly billing. Not a generalist. The role is scoped to what the buyer is short on: an outreach specialist, a digital PR lead, a HARO manager, or a content researcher for linkable-asset campaigns. From a top link-building agency without the recruiting cycle or the fully loaded cost.

White-Label partnership

Full top-notch link-building services delivery under an agency partner's brand, NDA-backed, no identity leak. Fits SEO agencies that want to offer multi-tactic link building without hiring an outreach team, a digital PR lead, and a content researcher in-house. Placement lists, drafts, live-URL logs, and reports ship branded as the partner's.

Transparency

Every placement URL, publisher name, anchor text, target page, and go-live date is disclosed to the buyer before the link goes live. No blind link deliveries. No 'we built twenty links this month, trust us' reports. Toxic-link watch runs monthly and any new referring domain that trips the toxicity screen is flagged in the report, not hidden. That is the difference between the best link building agency and a link seller.

How we deliver our link building services

The model exists so nothing depends on a single person’s memory or a shared inbox. Governance
and reporting are foregrounded, not added at the end.

Scope and discovery

The engagement opens with a scoping call to align on the buyer's target link profile: DR spread, anchor mix, referring-domain velocity, topical categories, competitor benchmark, and any exclusion list (competitor sites, sensitive verticals). A written scope document lands within three business days, covering monthly link target, tactic mix, publisher DR band, anchor plan, and reporting rhythm. Nothing starts, and no outreach is sent, without a signed scope.

What you receive: written scope document, named delivery lead, kick-off call scheduled, target link profile document.

Audit and baseline

Every link building services engagement begins with a two-part audit: a backlink profile audit of what the buyer has (referring domains, DR distribution, anchor text portfolio, toxic-link exposure), and a competitor gap audit against the top three ranking competitors per money page. The gap audit is what tells RCV Technologies which tactics to prioritize: heavy competitor DR gap points to editorial guest posting and digital PR, while heavy topical gap points to resource-page outreach and linkable-asset creation.

What you receive: written backlink audit, competitor gap report, toxic-link exposure score, prioritized tactic mix.

Execution

Certified specialists on the RCV Technologies bench execute against the scope in the cadence set at intake. Outreach specialists, digital PR leads, HARO managers, and content researchers are each capped by account load, so no one is stretched across ten campaigns at once. Pitching, drafting, editing, and placement happen inside Gobble, the internal delivery platform, so the delivery lead can see every publisher touchpoint, every journalist pitch, every draft, and every live URL at any point in the month.

What you receive: monthly placements per the scope, disclosed URLs and anchors before publish, tactic-mix report, from named specialists.

Reporting and review

Every month closes with a written report against the metrics agreed at intake: placements live, referring-domain lift, DR movement, anchor text mix progress, toxic-link changes, and profile-shape trajectory. Plus a monthly review call with the delivery lead. Every quarter closes with a quarterly business review that steps back from monthly counts to look at profile shape, competitor gap closure, ranking movement on target pages, and where the tactic mix should shift next quarter.

What you receive: monthly link profile report, monthly review call, quarterly profile-shape review with tactic-mix recommendation.

Iteration

The scope is not fixed for the life of the engagement, and neither is the tactic mix. At every quarterly review, tactic weights can be rebalanced (more digital PR, less guest posting, or vice versa), DR bands can be shifted, and anchor text mix can be re-tuned. The buyer is not locked into a link-building plan that the data contradicts.

What you receive: scope and tactic-mix adjustment options at every quarter, no lock-in beyond the contracted term.
