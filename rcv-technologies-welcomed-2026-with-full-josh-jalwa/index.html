Happy New Year

RCV Technologies Welcomed 2026 with Full Josh & Jalwa!

New Year means new beginnings, fresh goals, and unlimited energy,
and at RCV Technologies, we believe celebrations are not just events, they are emotions.

31st December: Celebration Ka Grand Kickstart

Office vibes truly electrified,

Music loud, smiles amplified,

Energy levels fully multiplied.

We kicked off our New Year celebrations right inside the office with an exciting flash mob, dance, and endless masti.

Har corner mein laughter, har beat pe dance, aur har face pe excitement, pura office ek mini dance floor ban gaya.

Aur waise bhi…

“Ye to starting hai bhai… aise kaise celebration khatam ho sakta hai?”

Because at RCV, Celebration Never Ends

RCVians believe that hard work deserves harder celebrations.

Team bonding, positivity, and happiness are part of our DNA, and this New Year was no exception.

3rd january: the ultimate club party

The celebration continued on 3rd January with a grand club party, and trust us, it was totally worth the wait!

  • Delicious food
  • Non-stop dancing
  • Chill vibes
  • Team laughter
  • Memories for lifetime

From grooving on the dance floor to enjoying mouth-watering food, every moment reflected the strong bond and vibrant culture we share at RCV Technologies.

These celebrations remind us that RCV Technologies is not just about projects and deadlines; it’s about people, passion, and progress.

We step into the New Year with:
  • New goals
  • New challenges
  • New dreams
  • And the same unbeatable team spirit
Cheers to 2026!

Here’s to another year of growth, success, innovation, and unforgettable celebrations.

Thank you to every RCVian who made these moments so special.

RCV Technologies wishes everyone a Happy New Year; let’s make 2026 legendary, together!
